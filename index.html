Coworking location candidates for Water Pros Plumbing

Three 5-mile areas cover every place on the list. Gretna and Chalco Hills is the best of the three.

Best area

Gretna and Chalco HillsCentered at 192nd St and Harrison St, reaching Gretna, Chalco Hills and Millard

  1. Least competition. 36 plumbers list a street address here, about 550 households earning $150k+ for each one. Northwest has 480 per plumber and Papillion 280.
  2. Most homeowners. 80% of homes are owner-occupied, against 67% in Northwest and 62% in Papillion. Homeowners are the ones who buy water heaters and softeners.
  3. Storage right by the center. Two new flex buildings with garage-door bays sit within about a mile, and one also offers outdoor storage.

Trade-offs: about 3,000 fewer $150k+ households than Northwest (19,700 against 22,800), and both flex buildings are new construction, so confirm move-in dates. If the timing doesn’t work, Northwest is the runner-up as the biggest market.

What to ask on each call

  1. Can the company name go on the suite door and on a building directory or sign? No listing says.
  2. What storage comes with the space, and what does it cost?
  3. Is the space on the ground floor, a short walk from parking?
  4. What is the total monthly cost, including NNN charges or expenses?
  5. Can Water Pros staff work there during the hours shown on Google, and will mail to the suite be accepted?

Google’s rule. Google accepts an office at a coworking space only if it has clear signage, receives customers during business hours, and is staffed by the business’s own staff during those hours. A flex unit that Water Pros leases and staffs meets this most easily.

How this was put together

Business. Water Pros Plumbing in monday.com (plumbing, contact Ryan) matches the Omaha business listed at 9802 Nicholas St, (402) 350-7782. Its review replies are signed by Ryan, and it describes itself as a veteran-owned water heater and water treatment specialist. The listed address is shown on the maps as a diamond.

Areas. Every point on a 0.1-mile grid was scored by households earning $150,000 or more within 5 miles. The three circles were chosen to reach the most of those households, each counted once, while covering all seven places on the list (northwest Omaha at Standing Bear Lake, West Omaha at Boys Town, Millard, Elkhorn, Chalco Hills, Papillion and Gretna, placed with OpenStreetMap). Each center was then moved to a nearby major intersection.

Best area. Gretna and Chalco Hills ranks first on $150k+ households per plumber and on homeowner share, and has two flex buildings with storage near the center. Northwest is larger but has more plumbers and only one office that lists storage.

Households. U.S. Census Bureau American Community Survey 2020 to 2024 5-year estimates by census tract, from the Esri Living Atlas ACS layers: B19001 (household income), B25003 (owner-occupied homes) and B25034 (year built). Each tract’s households were spread evenly over its land, with water removed, and summed within each circle.

Competitors. YellowPages Plumbers listings for Omaha and nearby towns, collected in November 2026 and located with the Census geocoder. BBB, the usual source, blocked access. Only plumbing businesses with a street address were counted, with duplicates merged.

Offices. Listings on CommercialCafe, CommercialSearch, Crexi, CBRE, Tenantbase and Office Hub, checked in November 2026. Storage and entry are rated only from what the listings say. Photos come from each listing.

Map. Census TIGERweb tracts, roads and water. Distances are straight-line miles.
